- `oldbalanceOrg`

- `type`

The target variable is `isFraud`.

## 6. Important Evaluation Caveat

Observed fraud prevalence differs substantially across the chronological partitions:

| Partition | Fraud Cases | Fraud Prevalence |
|---|---:|---:|
| Training | 3,643 | 0.081616% |
| Validation | 564 | 0.057527% |
| Test | 4,006 | 0.436090% |

Test-set fraud prevalence is approximately 7.6 times the validation-set prevalence.

Precision and Average Precision should therefore be interpreted in the context of this distribution shift. These results do not establish performance on real-world banking transactions or establish the cause of the prevalence change.
